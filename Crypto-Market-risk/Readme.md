## Market Risk of a crypto portfolio

* Volatility modeling (Garch, GJR-Garch) and conditional Var with Kupiec backtesting for BTC,ETH,BNB,SOL and XRP.

* This a just an academic work made that tends to enhance skills and understanding of a finance model, it's not a model that claims accuracy at 100% and can be used.
* The results are in-sample only, there are no out of sample testing.
* if you're just someone who's curious about finance models, don't think of using this model to take a real decision (if you might think i'll take this, give it to ai and enhance it...etc).
